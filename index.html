EarnIt

Help & Support

EarnIt turns focus into rewards. Every full minute of focus earns a coin. Spend your coins on rewards you choose yourself, and look back on everything you've done and earned. EarnIt is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Getting Started

1

Plan with Areas, Projects and Tasks

Group your life into Areas such as Work, Study or Health, break each Area into projects, and each project into tasks. Drag cards to reorder them or move a project to another Area.

2

Focus and Earn Coins

Tap the timer icon on a task, or pick a task on the Focus tab, and start. Stop and tap ✓ to save. Every full minute earns 1 coin; sessions shorter than 1 minute are not recorded.

3

Focus Anywhere

A running session appears as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can also start a session on Apple Watch, or from the menu bar on Mac.

4

Reward Yourself in the Store

Create your own rewards and set their price. Add them to your cart and watch the progress grow as you focus. Pay with the coins of one Area or your total balance.

5

Look Back in History

See every focus session, finished task and reward on one timeline, or switch to By Area. Add photos to purchases, and export your history as an image.

6

Check Your Stats and Widgets

Your profile shows focus time and coins for each Area, with daily to yearly trends. Add widgets to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, or complications to your Apple Watch face.

FAQ

Is EarnIt free?

Yes. You can use all features for free with up to 3 Areas, 3 Projects and 3 rewards. EarnIt Pro (monthly, yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase) removes these limits.

Does my purchase work on all my devices?

Yes. EarnIt Pro bought on iPhone, iPad or Mac works on all of them when you're signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → EarnIt → Cancel Subscription. On Mac: System Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Subscriptions → Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. Open the EarnIt Pro screen (profile → Settings → plan, or Settings in the Mac sidebar) and tap "Restore Purchase" while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How is my data saved and synced?

Your data is saved on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your personal iCloud account. It syncs automatically across devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Apple Watch syncs through your iPhone.

Will my data be lost if I delete the app?

Not if iCloud is on. Reinstall EarnIt and sign in with the same Apple Account, and your data is restored from iCloud.

Why wasn't my focus session saved?

Sessions shorter than 1 minute are not recorded, because they don't earn a coin. The app shows a message when this happens.

How do I use EarnIt from the Mac menu bar?

While EarnIt is running, click its icon in the menu bar, pick a task and press play. The menu bar shows the running time. Stop and click ✓ to save; the main window opens with the new session.

How do I export my history?

Open History and tap the download button to save an image to Photos. The first time, choose "Allow" when asked to add photos.

使用指南

1

用领域、项目和任务做计划

用领域整理生活，例如工作、学习、健康；把领域拆成项目，把项目拆成任务。拖动卡片可以调整顺序，或把项目移到其他领域。

2

专注并赚取金币

点击任务上的计时图标，或在「专注」页选择任务，然后开始。结束后点击 ✓ 保存。每专注满 1 分钟获得 1 金币；少于 1 分钟的专注不会记录。

3

随时随地专注

专注进行中会显示在锁定屏幕的实时活动和灵动岛中。也可以在 Apple Watch 上开始专注，或在 Mac 的菜单栏中专注。

4

在商店奖励自己

自定义奖励并设定价格，加入购物车，随着专注看着进度一点点增长。可以用某个领域的金币支付，也可以用总余额支付。

5

在历史记录中回顾

在一条时间线上查看每次专注、完成的任务和奖励，或切换为按领域查看。可以给购买记录添加照片，还能把历史记录导出为图片。

6

查看统计和小组件

个人页显示每个领域的专注时长和金币，以及日、周、月、年的专注趋势。还可以在主屏幕或锁定屏幕添加小组件，或在 Apple Watch 表盘添加复杂功能。

常见问题

EarnIt 免费吗？

免费。所有功能都可以免费使用，最多 3 个领域、3 个项目和 3 个奖励。EarnIt Pro（月度、年度订阅或一次性永久购买）不限数量。

购买后所有设备都能用吗？

可以。使用同一个 Apple 账户，在 iPhone、iPad 或 Mac 上购买的 EarnIt Pro 在所有设备上都有效。

如何取消自动续费？

iPhone 或 iPad：「设置」→ 你的名字 →「订阅」→ EarnIt →「取消订阅」。Mac：「系统设置」→ 你的名字 →「媒体与购买项目」→「订阅」→「管理」。请在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则会自动续订。

购买后能否恢复？

可以。打开 EarnIt Pro 页面（个人页 →「设置」→ 方案；Mac 上为侧边栏的「设置」），使用同一个 Apple 账户点击「恢复购买」即可。

数据怎样保存和同步？

数据保存在你的设备上；如果开启了 iCloud，也会保存在你个人的 iCloud 账户中，并在登录同一 Apple 账户的设备之间自动同步。Apple Watch 通过 iPhone 同步。

卸载 App 后数据会丢失吗？

开启了 iCloud 就不会。重新安装 EarnIt 并登录同一个 Apple 账户，数据会从 iCloud 自动恢复。

为什么专注没有被记录？

少于 1 分钟的专注不会记录，因为不足以获得金币。出现这种情况时 App 会给出提示。

如何在 Mac 菜单栏中专注？

EarnIt 运行时，点击菜单栏中的 EarnIt 图标，选择任务并点击开始，菜单栏会显示专注时长。结束后点击 ✓ 保存，主窗口会打开并显示新的专注记录。

如何导出历史记录？

打开历史记录，点击下载按钮即可把图片保存到相册。第一次保存时，请在询问时选择「允许」添加照片。
